Shamanism is arguably humanity's oldest psycho-technology - the systematic use of practices that alter and enhance the _software_ of cognition without changing its hardware. Emerging around the [[Upper Paleolithic Transition]], shamanic practice deployed sleep deprivation, extended singing, dancing and chanting, social isolation, imitation through masks and costume, and sometimes psychedelics to induce altered states of consciousness. [[John Vervaeke]] frames these not as primitive superstition but as disciplined tools for cognitive enhancement, drawing on Andy Clark's image of humans as "natural-born cyborgs": just as removing literacy from a mind collapses the range of problems it can solve, shamanic techniques _expanded_ that range - same brain, upgraded software.

The etymology already ties the figure to [[Wisdom]]: "shaman" means one who knows, sees, has insight - a direct parallel to "wizard," the wise one. The shaman combined roles that later civilisations distributed across separate specialists: charismatic performer, artist, therapist, healer - able, among other things, to trigger the recipient's own placebo response, which accounts for a substantial share of the efficacy of real medicine even today. In this sense the shaman stands at the headwaters of the [[contemplative traditions]], [[mysticism]], and [[ritual magic]] alike.

Most significantly, the shaman is a _[[participatory]]_ knower. "Becoming the deer" - taking on the animal's perspective and way of being to enhance tracking - is not a metaphysical claim but a mode of knowing through [[participation]] rather than detached observation. This anticipates the participatory epistemologies that thinkers like Vervaeke and [[Richard Tarnas]] argue modernity lost, and whose absence feeds [[the meaning crisis]]. Shamanic practice also served the social fabric: alongside [[Ritual]] and [[initiation]], it helped bind groups into networks of [[Distributed Cognition]], extending trust and shared sense-making beyond the kinship group.

The shamanic pattern - descent into darkness, ordeal, transformation, return with medicine - prefigures the [[The Heros Journey]] and the deep structure of [[initiation]] everywhere. Its states border on what [[magical consciousness]] describes, and its disruptive strategies for inducing [[awakening]] experiences (per Steve Taylor's _Waking from Sleep_) remain recognisable in contemplative and psychedelic practice today. Understanding shamanism means recognising that the link between altered states, meaning-making, and enhanced contact with the world is not a modern discovery but the original human inheritance.
